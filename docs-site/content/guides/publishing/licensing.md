# Licensing and publication rights

The generator's software license, bundled dependencies, and your content are
separate considerations. Review the applicable terms before distributing the
executable or publishing a site.

## Generator and bundled software

This project uses the same SEI Design System license as SDS Native and the
SEI Design System. The full
[project license](https://github.com/cmu-sei/sds-site-generator/blob/main/LICENSE)
is included as `LICENSE` in npm packages and standalone release archives.
It is a custom license; do not assume MIT or Apache terms.

The license describes use of generated code under your product's chosen terms
with additional acknowledgments and relevant third-party obligations.
Read the full text rather than treating this summary as a grant of rights.
For permission questions, the license lists `permission@sei.cmu.edu`.

SDS Lite, Lucide icons, syntax highlighting, and other dependencies retain
their applicable terms. Generated sites include design-system notices at
`_site/sds-lite/LICENSE.txt` and code-control/highlighting notices at
`_site/code-controls-LICENSE.txt`. Keep the generated acknowledgments and
license files when publishing. Redistributed executables must also retain
the supplied project and third-party notices.

## Your text, images, and attachments

Using the generator does not grant permission to publish someone else's
content or automatically apply the generator's license to your original work.
Before adding an image, excerpt, example, or downloadable file:

- Confirm that you own it or have the necessary permission/license.
- Follow required attribution, notice, and redistribution conditions.
- Review confidential information, personal data, and internal-only material.
- Remember that assets are published even when no page links to them.

Source-code examples and imported documentation may carry their own notices.
Preserve those notices and seek appropriate review when rights are unclear.

## Publish your own content terms

If readers need explicit reuse terms, add a normal Markdown page describing
the terms approved for your content and link it from your navigation or pages.
That page does not override third-party licenses or grant rights you do not
hold. The generator does not choose a content license or enforce licensing.

For private sites, licensing is not access control: protect the complete
deployment at the host as described in
[team workflows](team-workflows.md#drafts-hidden-pages-and-private-documentation).
